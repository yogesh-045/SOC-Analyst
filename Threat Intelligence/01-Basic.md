# 1. What is Threat Intelligence? ⭐

## Definition

**Threat Intelligence (TI)** is the process of collecting, analyzing, and interpreting information about cyber threats to understand:

- Who is attacking
- What they are targeting
- How they attack
- What Indicators of Compromise (IOCs) they use
- How the organization can detect and prevent the attack

In simple words:

> **Threat Intelligence converts raw threat data into useful security information that helps an organization detect, prevent, and respond to cyber attacks.**

---

## Purpose of Threat Intelligence

The main purposes of Threat Intelligence are:

1. **Identify Threats**
   - Understand current and emerging cyber threats.

2. **Improve Detection**
   - Use threat information to create better SIEM and EDR detections.

3. **Prevent Attacks**
   - Block malicious IPs, domains, URLs, hashes, and other known indicators.

4. **Understand Attackers**
   - Learn about threat actors, their motivations, tools, and techniques.

5. **Support Incident Response**
   - Help analysts investigate and understand security incidents faster.

6. **Reduce Risk**
   - Identify potential threats before they cause serious damage.

7. **Improve Security Decisions**
   - Provide actionable intelligence to security teams and management.

---
