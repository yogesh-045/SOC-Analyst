# 6. Threat Intelligence Lifecycle 

The **Threat Intelligence Lifecycle** is a continuous process used to collect, process, analyze, and distribute threat intelligence.

## 1. Planning

Define:

- What intelligence is required?
- Which threats need to be monitored?
- Who needs the intelligence?
- What are the intelligence requirements?

**Goal:** Define the objectives and requirements.

---

## 2. Collection

Collect raw threat data from different sources.

### Sources

- Threat Intelligence Feeds
- OSINT
- SIEM
- EDR
- Firewall Logs
- Malware Reports
- Security Researchers
- Dark Web Monitoring

**Goal:** Gather relevant threat data.

---

## 3. Processing

Convert raw data into a usable and structured format.

### Activities

- Remove duplicate data
- Normalize data
- Validate information
- Extract IOCs
- Organize threat data

**Goal:** Prepare collected data for analysis.

---

## 4. Analysis

Analyze the processed data to identify meaningful intelligence.

### Activities

- Identify threats
- Analyze IOCs
- Analyze TTPs
- Identify threat actors
- Determine risk and impact
- Correlate information

**Goal:** Convert raw data into actionable intelligence.

---

## 5. Dissemination

Share the analyzed intelligence with the appropriate teams.

### Consumers

- SOC Analysts
- Incident Response Team
- Threat Hunters
- Security Engineers
- CISO / Management

### Examples

- Threat Intelligence Reports
- SIEM Rules
- IOC Feeds
- Security Alerts
- Threat Briefings

**Goal:** Deliver the right intelligence to the right people.

---

## 6. Feedback

Collect feedback from intelligence consumers and use it to improve the next cycle.

### Examples

- Was the intelligence useful?
- Was it accurate?
- Did it help detect a threat?
- What additional intelligence is required?

**Goal:** Improve the intelligence process continuously.
